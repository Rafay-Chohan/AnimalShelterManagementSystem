# AnimalShelterManagementSystem
 <p>Designed a management system for a rescue shelter using PHP, MySQL for backend, and HTML/CSS for frontend. The app includes features like: <b><br>Sign Up (Management/Public):</b></br>  Management and public will be registered along their respective bio’s<br><b>Log In (Management/Public):</b></br> Admin(Management) and users(public) will be able to login with valid credentials<br><b>Profile Management:</b></br> Option for both Admin(Management) and users(public) will be able to manage their profile<br><b>Adoption:</b></br> Here the Public can see which animals are up for adoption(Data would be taken from Database)<br><b>Foster:</b></br> Here the Public can see which animals are up for Foster (Data would be taken from Database)<br><b>Sponsor:</b></br> Here the Public can see which animals are up for Sponsor (Data would be taken from Database)<br><b>Report a case:</b></br> Here the public can report a case to our team then our team will handle the rescue<br><b>Portfolio:</b></br> Here the shelter can add their previously completed projects<br><b>Current Projects:</b></br> Here the Shelter upcoming Projects will be shown and people can chose to help by volunteering as well<br><b>Upcoming Events:</b></br> Here the upcoming Fundraising Events will be shown so the supporters can come and show their support<br><b>Register for Volunteer:</b></br> Here People can register for volunteering their contact info etc would be saved and so they will be contacted whenever a new volunteer cycle is about to start.</p>
